Chaski is a calm, modern RSS/Atom feed reader. No ads, no telemetry, and no data leaves your device.

<ul>
<li>Auto-discovery of RSS/Atom feeds from a website URL</li>
<li>Clean, distraction-free interface with dark/light modes and multiple themes</li>
<li>Import and export feeds in OPML format</li>
<li>Organize feeds with accounts and folders</li>
<li>Custom filters per feed</li>
<li>Notifications for new entries</li>
<li>Configurable reading fonts</li>
<li>Local accounts, plus Google Reader API accounts such as FreshRSS, Miniflux and TinyTiny RSS</li>
<li>Full-text search across your articles</li>
<li>Read full articles inside the app</li>
<li>Offline mode: save articles for later and they are downloaded for you</li>
</ul>
